# Surface: the game table (index.html)

Mode: Experience. Desktop only. Players are inside the game; the throw leads from the first viewport.

Audience and job: friends at one desktop (or one player vs CPU) taking turns to throw two wolf dice, then choosing roll or bank. Success: the throw feels physical, the result and whose turn it is read in under a second.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The screen is a cabin game-night table seen from your chair: the wool blanket is the arena and the pine pegboard is the scoreboard. It refuses the casual-mobile-game default of bright flat panels and floating rounded buttons.

OWN-WORLD: Red-and-black buffalo-check wool (#9e1b1b / #1a1714) owns the field; knotty pine (#c9965b) frames it as a tray rim and as the pegboard; cream (#e9dcc4) for lettering; brass pegs. Alfa Slab One for routed/burned lettering, Figtree for small UI text. Saturated colour beyond wool and wood is reserved for the active seat's peg lane and banner.

STORY: Players see the wolves waiting at their edge, drag to throw, watch them tumble, read the pose in big slab type, and decide roll or bank. The pegboard shows the whole race to the target at once, every empty hole drawn.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed 3D blanket in a pine tray seen three-quarter from the player's edge. Bottom: a full-width pine pegboard strip, one lane per seat, holes in groups of ten. Top-left: active player plate (name, banked, turn pot). Bottom-centre above the board: Roll and Bank as burned pine tabs. Result line centred over the blanket.

FORM: Cabin Game Night, position 5 of 7 on the grounded list. seed:9e33369f0a08dc8c

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Drag on the blanket to sling the wolves; release throws. Turn handover sweeps a seat-coloured band across the table with the next player's name.

Raises: unpegged holes drawn deliberately (from seven-segment); saturated colour only for the active seat (from glazier); complete keyboard play and text results (from ANSI BBS).
